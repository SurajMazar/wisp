# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.2 (unreleased)
Everything after the first alpha, built in Phases 9–21:
- **Chat** in the style of modern assistants: live progress steps, streamed reasoning ("Thought for Ns"),
  model picker, quick actions, copy/regenerate/read aloud, grouped history.
- **Screen understanding**: on-device text recognition, budgeted context, text-only fallback, a "Context" disclosure.
- **Visual guidance**: answers highlight what they mean on screen and the cursor buddy flies there.
- **Computer control** behind risk analysis, approvals, verification and an Activity log (off by default).
- **Teacher mode**: "Teach me how to…" step-by-step lessons that watch for each step.
- **Agents** with plans, limits, loop detection and live run cards; **Skills** (SKILL.md); **Memory** (explicit,
  with Undo); **Files** with cited answers; **Routines** on schedules with notifications.
- **Security hardening**, **performance** work, a first-run setup, backups, export and update checks.

## 0.1.0-alpha.1
Foundation through voice: host app, engine, IPC, settings, invocation, assistant cursor, focus capture,
Accessibility, AI providers (Ollama, Apple, OpenAI-compatible), chat, push-to-talk with Whisper and Kokoro.
